Notes[]

  • This fight is no longer capped at 25; it is Level Restriction 99. Alter Egos may be summoned inside the BCNM.
  • If you did mission 2-2, you may need to trade 1 crystal to a Temple Knight, or do Bat Hunt once.
  • If you skipped mission 2-2, you may need to trade 3 crystals for rank points.

Walkthrough[]

  1. Receive the mission from a San d'Orian Gate Guard.
  2. Northern San d'Oria (I-7) - Enter the Chateau.
  3. Chateau d'Oraguille - Talk to Halver to receive a Letter to the Consuls.
    • He'll tell you to travel to Bastok and Windurst to visit your nation's embassies.
      • There are two possible walkthroughs, depending on if you visit Bastok first or Windurst first.
      • The Bastok-first walkthrough is listed first. Scroll down for the Windurst-first walkthrough.
    • If you are in possession of Rhapsody in White, you will also receive Cipher: Halver.

(BasW) Bastok Windurst Walkthrough[]

(BasW) Bastok[]

Mission Name Journey to Bastok
Number 2-3
Items Needed Pickaxes
Mine Gravel
Mythril Sand



Suggestion: Buy a stack of Pickaxes from shops, to save you the trip of returning to Bastok to purchase more; pickaxes can break when mining and Mine Gravel (you need one) is a high drop, but not guaranteed, per mine attempt. You can also buy Mine Gravel (AH: Others > Misc), but you have to travel to Palborough either way, so it is not worth it usually.

If you've never been to Bastok, you can Unity warp to South Gustaberg ("Content level 99 and below") and head to:

  1. Bastok Markets (H-7) - Enter the Metalworks.
  2. Metalworks (I-9) - Go in and take a lift to the second floor, and go to the Consulate of San d'Oria (Bastok).
    • Speak to Savae E Paleade for a cutscene.
  3. Metalworks (J-8) - Continue east of the Consulate to enter the Department of Industry.
    • Go up the little stairs and in. It is the door to the right of the President's Office.
    • Speak to Pius.
  4. Metalworks (G-9) - Walk back west toward the lifts to reach the Craftsmen's Eatery, south of CId's workshop.
    • Speak to Grohm for a cutscene. He will give you 3 Pickaxes.
    • Grohm will describe the general process of how to get Mine Gravel & Mythril Sand.
  5. North Gustaberg (K-3) - Go around the west side of Zegham Hill to enter Palborough Mines in the north.
    • If you are low level, you may want to bring Silent Oils to prevent aggro from the level 21-23 Quadav on the 3rd floor.
  6. Palborough Mines (F-7) (1st floor) or (I-9) (3rd floor) - Locate a Mythril Seam and trade a pickaxe to it.
    • It may take several attempts at the same seam to obtain Mine Gravel.
    • If you receive the message "You find a chunk of mine gravel, but your pickaxe breaks in the process," you do successfully get the mine gravel. Just check your inventory.
    • Each Mine Gravel consumes one inventory slot.
  7. Palborough Mines (I-8) - Take the elevator to the third floor.
  8. Palborough Mines (I-6) - Trade Mine Gravel to Refiner Lid.
    • Pull the lever on the refiner to activate it.
    • Drop off the ledge to the floor below you.
    • Pull the second lever. Mythril Sand will be put into your inventory automatically.
      • If you forgot to hit the lever after trading the Mine Gravel on the 3rd floor, you have to make a short (1min) run back around and up from the 2nd floor.
  9. Palborough Mines Floor3 (H-8) - Just around the corner from the elevator, you can take a boat to Zeruhn Mines (H-6) and quickly return to Bastok Mines.
  10. Metalworks (I-9) - Return to Savae E Paleade and trade her the Mythril Sand to complete the Bastok leg of the mission.

(BasW) Windurst (after Bastok)[]

Mission Name Journey to Windurst
Number 2-3
Items Needed Dark Key



  1. If this is your first time heading to Windurst, you might go this route:
    • Boat from Selbina to Mhaura -> Buburimu (D-7) -> Tahrongi (H-13) -> East Sarutabaruta (G-11) = Windurst Woods.
  2. Windurst Woods (F-10) - Talk to Mourices at the Consulate of San d'Oria (Windurst). Then head north to Windurst Walls.
  3. Windurst Walls (H-7) - Enter Heavens Tower.
  4. Heavens Tower (H-6) - Talk to Kupipi (in the clerical chamber straight ahead from entrance) to receive a Dark Key. Make sure you do not skip this step!
    • Everyone in your party must have this item to enter the BC, or have completed 2-3 previously in their current nation of allegiance. One person cannot get others into the fight who do not have the Dark Key, even if they are currently on this part of the mission, unless they have completed the mission previously.
    • If you are in possession of "Rhapsody in White", you will also receive Cipher: Semih.
    • If she gives you Trust: Kupipi, keep talking to her until you trigger the 2-3 mission cutscene.
  5. West Sarutabaruta (F-8) - Enter Giddeus.
    • To get there, you can exit from Windurst Waters (Home Point #1) or Port Windurst.
    • Only Invisible is needed in Giddeus. Buffs are removed when you enter the BCNM.
  6. Giddeus (G-12) - Enter Balga's Dais.
    • Follow the west wall until you reach a cave at (G-7).
    • Keep following the west wall except when it goes down at (G-8). You want to exit the cave not go down.
      • Avoid the grass or you might fall down to the lower floor.
    • Cut diagonally across the open area.
    • When you enter the tight path, start following the east wall until you find Balga's Dais.
  7. Balga's Dais - Enter the Burning Circle for a cutscene and to enter the BCNM with Searcher and Black Dragon.
    • If you are solo, make sure you are a minimum of level 30 and on a very strong job, such as a Monk using Hundred Fists. Kill the dragon before killing Searcher, or the dragon will kill you rather easily after Searcher has weakened you. Searcher does not melee you unless you are close to it or it has less than 50% of its HP, so it is relatively inactive if you focus on the dragon first.
    • If in a party with a Black Mage, the recommended order is to kill Searcher, then Black Dragon. The Black Mage should use Elemental Seal + Sleep on the dragon while everyone kills Searcher. Once Searcher is gone, the Black Dragon will die quickly. This dragon has a very potent AOE poison attack that does 10 damage per tick, which makes antidotes necessary. He also may curse the tanks.(see testimonials)
  8. Once the Black Dragon is defeated, another cutscene will happen. When it finishes, you will receive the Kindred crest.
    • A glitch may prevent this cutscene from occurring. If this happens, you can view the cutscene here (begin at 8:53). Alternatively, you can exit the dais and reenter from the main entrance to watch from the Goblin Footprint.
  9. West Sarutabaruta - Exit the zone into West Saruta. Return to Windurst.
  10. Windurst Woods (F-10) - Report back to Mourices at the embassy to receive a Kindred Report.
  11. Northern San d'Oria (I-7) - Enter Chateau d'Oraguille and report back to Halver to complete the mission.

Additional Information:

After completing the mission, you should probably go talk to Curilla to obtain her Alter-Ego at this point.

(WinB) Windurst Bastok Walkthrough[]

(WinB) Windurst[]

Mission Name Journey to Windurst
Number 2-3
Items Needed Shield Offering
Parana Shield x2



  • Head to the Consulate of San d'Oria (Windurst) in Windurst Woods at (G-10) and talk to Mourices.
  • Enter Heavens Tower in Windurst Walls at (H-7) for a cutscene.
  • Enter the Clerical Chamber and speak to Kupipi. She will give you a Shield Offering.
    • If you are in possession of "Rhapsody in White", you will also receive Cipher: Semih.
  • Head to Giddeus in West Sarutabaruta at (F-8).
  • After entering Giddeus, you must defeat Zhuu Buxu the Silent twice to obtain 2 Parana Shields.
    • To get to Zhuu Buxu the Silent, head to the path at (I-8) on the first map and go down the tunnels. Then, make your way to (H-7) on the second map.
    • Zhuu Buxu the Silent can aggro and link with the surrounding Yagudo.
    • The respawn time for Zhuu Buxu the Silent is 5 minutes.
  • Make your way to (F-7) on the second map and speak to Uu Zhoumo to give the Shield Offering. You can do this while waiting for Zhuu Buxu the Silent to respawn.
  • Return to the Consulate of San d'Oria and speak to Mourices. Trade him both Parana Shields as proof of your completed task.

(WinB) Bastok[]

Mission Name Journey to Bastok
Number 2-3



  • Head to the Consulate of San d'Oria (Bastok) in the Metalworks at (H-9) on the second floor and speak to Savae E Paleade for a cutscene.
  • Enter the Department of Industry adjacent to the President's Office and speak to Pius.
  • Walk to the Craftsmen's Eatery at (G-9) and speak to Grohm.
  • Head to the Palborough Mines located in North Gustaberg at (K-3).
  • In the Palborough Mines, make your way to the third floor of the mines and enter the Waughroon Shrine.
    • If you are low level, you may want to bring Silent Oils to prevent aggro from the level 21-23 Quadav.
    • The Waughroon Shrine is located at (H-10) on the third floor.
  • Enter the Burning Circle for a cutscene and to enter the BCNM with Seeker and Dark Dragon.
    • If you are solo, make sure you are a minimum of level 30 and on a very strong job, such as a Monk using Hundred Fists. A solo hero with 4 trusts should have little difficulty clearing this BCNM below level 30.
  • Defeat the two monsters to obtain a Kindred crest.
    • A glitch can prevent the victory cutscene from occuring. If that happens, you can watch the cutscene here: [1] (begin watching from 1:53), or you can exit the shrine and reenter from the main entrance to watch from the Goblin Footprint.
  • After you've won the BCNM, you can exit back into Palborough Mines, travel a short distance on that map to H-8, where you can board one of the lever-activated ferries and return to Zeruhn Mines (which is connected to Bastok Mines).
  • Return to Bastok and speak to Savae E Paleade to obtain the Kindred Report and to complete this portion of the mission.
  • Return to San d'Oria and speak to Halver to complete the mission.

=END OF WinB=== Game Description ==

Mission Orders
Visit the faraway nations of Bastok and Windurst and receive official recognition as an adventurer.
Mission Orders (Windurst to Bastok)
Journey to Windurst
Bring an offering (a shield) from Heavens Tower in Windurst to the Yagudo treasure room in Giddeus. To uphold the honor of San d'Oria, claim two of the same shields from the Yagudo on your return.
Journey to Bastok
Reports indicate that the Quadav who inhabit the deepest levels of Palborough Mines are up to something. An official named Pius will instruct you further.
Mission Orders (Bastok to Windurst)
Journey to Bastok
Survey Bastokan mining technology, and bring some mythril sand out of Palborough Mines. An official named Pius will instruct you further.
Journey to Windurst
Oust the giant monster that dwells deep inside the Yagudo realm of Giddeus.
