Walkthrough[]

  • You must complete several quests to collect four Large Memory Fragments.
    • You will need a Lilac (NPC upper Jeuno), a Philosopher's Stone (AH>Materials>Alchemy), Rice Vinegar, Holy Basil, Ground Wasabi (NPC Lower Jeuno Tenshodo)
    • TIP: You can complete the quests in any desired order. (However it is best to start the second fragment first due to game day wait in the quest.)
  • The next mission is automatically flagged once the below quests are completed.
  • CLICK EACH QUEST FOR DETAILED INFO

The First Fragment[]

  • Her Memories: Homecoming Queen
    • Area: Southern San d'Oria
      • Zone into Southern San d'Oria from East Ronfaure to begin the quest.
        • Home Point #2 is closest to the East Ronfaure zone line.
      • This quest comprises of the following three sub-quests, which each bestow a Tiny Memory Fragment. Once the three have been completed, they will automatically form a Large Memory Fragment and complete this quest.
    • Her Memories: Grave Resolve
      • Area: Chateau d'Oraguille
        • Talk to Halver in Chateau d'Oraguille at (I-9) to begin the quest.
          • Item needed: Lilac
    • Her Memories: The Faux Pas
      • Area: Northern San d'Oria
        • Talk to Abioleget inside the Cathedral in Northern San d'Oria at the north border of (M-7) to begin the quest.
    • Her Memories: Old Bean
      • Area: Port San d'Oria
        • Talk to Thierride inside the Rusty Anchor Pub in Port San d'Oria at (G-7) to begin the quest.

The Second Fragment[]

  • Her Memories: Of Malign Maladies
    • Area: Southern San d'Oria
      • Talk to Amaura in Southern San d'Oria at (G-6) to begin the quest.
        • Item needed: Philosopher's Stone

The Third Fragment[]

  • Her Memories: Operation Cupid
    • Area: Batallia Downs (S)
      • Zone into Batallia Downs (S) from Jugner Forest (S) to begin the quest.
        • Items needed: Rice Vinegar, Holy Basil, Ground Wasabi
        • Using the Survival Guide in Batallia Downs (S) will put you next to Jugner Forest (S) zone line.

The Fourth Fragment[]

  • One of the below quests must be completed in order to receive the Large memory fragment (IV).
    • Please note that you can only activate and complete the quest associated with the past nation you were allied to at the time that you flagged this mission.

If you were enlisted in the Cobra Mercenaries[]

  • Her Memories: Verdure Footfalls
    • Area: Windurst Waters (S)
      • Warp items are recommended to help save time.
      • Zone into Windurst Waters (S) from West Sarutabaruta (S) for a cutscene, then talk to Miah Riyuh for a cutscene to begin the quest.

If you were enlisted in the Iron Rams[]

  • Her Memories: Carnelian Footfalls
    • Area: Southern San d'Oria (S)
      • Zone into Southern San d'Oria (S) from East Ronfaure (S) for a cutscene, then talk to Mainchelite at (I-9) for a cutscene to begin the quest.

If you were enlisted in the Fourth Division[]

  • Her Memories: Azure Footfalls
    • Area: Bastok Markets (S)
      • Zone into Bastok Markets (S) from North Gustaberg (S) for a cutscene, then talk to Adelbrecht at (E-8) for a cutscene to begin the quest.

Fragments of Lilisette's memories lie scattered across the land. In retracing the footsteps of her past, the way may be revealed to you.
