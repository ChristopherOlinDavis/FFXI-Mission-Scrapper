Walkthrough[]

  • Totoroon (near Nashmau Home Point #1) asks you to bring him "Sweets, with shooogary taste."
  • Trade Totoroon a Date, Pamamas or Kazham Pineapple.
  • He will send you to one of four places: Bhaflau Thickets, Wajaom Woodlands, Caedarva Mire or Mount Zhayolm.
    • You will be sent to Bhaflau Thickets or Wajaom Woodlands for trading a Date.
    • You will be sent to Wajaom Woodlands or Bhaflau Thickets for trading Pamamas.
    • You will be sent to Mount Zhayolm or Caedarva Mire for trading a Kazham Pineapple.
  • Once you obtain the item, speak to Totoroon to complete the quest.

Bhaflau Thickets[]

  • In Bhaflau Thickets, you will need to find a harvesting point.
  • Using a Sickle may result in an item from the list below.
  • Berried Chigoe and some Chigoe's Nits may spawn as a result of Harvesting while this quest is active.
    • Killing the NM is not required.
    • If the NM is defeated, it drops an item from the list below.
    • Any Harvesting done after the NM is killed will yield normal Bhaflau Thickets/Harvesting results.
