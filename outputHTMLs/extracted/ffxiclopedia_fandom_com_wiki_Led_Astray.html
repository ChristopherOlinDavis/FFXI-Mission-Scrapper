Start NPC Mhasbaf - Aht Urhgan Whitegate (J-8)
Repeatable No
Reward 1 Imperial Silver Piece



Walkthrough[]

  • Talk to Mhasbaf in Aht Urhgan Whitegate at J-8.
  • Head to the lower section of J-8 and approach the pillar for a cutscene.
  • Talk to Tohka Telposkha at I-8 for a cutscene; tell the Galka to go to Port Ephramad.
If you select any other option you'll have to repeat your selection until you choose Port Ephramad.
  • Repeat this for Rubahah at F-9, then Cacaroon at G-11.
  • Go to I-11, slightly down the ramp beside the ferry dock, for a cutscene and a Letter from Bernahn.
  • Talk to Tataroon at G-8 in Nashmau for the final cutscene and your reward.
