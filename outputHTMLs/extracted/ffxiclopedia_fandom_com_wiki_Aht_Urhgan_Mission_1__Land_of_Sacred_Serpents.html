Walkthrough[]

  • Obtain a Boarding Permit from either the quest The Road to Aht Urhgan in Lower Jeuno or the Rhapsodies of Vana'diel mission Inescapable Binds.
  • Board the ferry from Mhaura to the Empire of Aht Urhgan.
    • It is suggested to immediately buy the map from the map vendor Riyadahf, who is north of where you enter Aht Urhgan.
    • You can bypass the boat by Unity warping to Wajaom Woodlands. Then use the Postern at M-7 to arrive below Salaheem's Sentinels. Note that you still need the Boarding Permit.
  • Enter Salaheem's Sentinels in Aht Urhgan Whitegate (Home Point #1) (top floor of I-10) for a cutscene and a Supplies Package.

Note[]

This mission alone does not fulfill the Records of Eminence quest "Treasures of Aht Urhgan 1". For that, you must complete the Aht Urhgan Missions up to the end of Aht Urhgan Mission 7: Westerly Winds.
