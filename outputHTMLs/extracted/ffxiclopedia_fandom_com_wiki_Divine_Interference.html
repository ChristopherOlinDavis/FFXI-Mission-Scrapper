Walkthrough[]

  • Wait until Japanese Midnight has passed and enter Salaheem's Sentinels. The cutscene for Divine Interference begins automatically as you get near/pass Abquhbah.
    • Talking to Naja Salaheem can also start the Unwavering Resolve quest.
  • Examine the Imperial Whitegate door (L-9) for a cutscene.
    • If you get a cutscene for an Odin Prime quest, recheck the Imperial Whitegate.
    • After checking the Imperial Whitegate for this quest and accepting it, you can recheck the Imperial Whitegate to start Unwavering Resolve.
      • Quest does not appear in your log until you complete this step.
  • From here on, this quest is similar to Waking the Colossus without the three nations part.
    • Go to Mount Zhayolm for a cutscene at the Acid-Eaten Door (I-9) far south on the second floor of the Cerberus spawn area.
    • Get some pickaxes and mine 3 slabs of Plumbago in Mount Zhayolm or Halvung.
    • Plumbago also drops from Troll Ironworker and Troll Lapidarist in Halvung.
    • Plumbago also drops from Hilltroll Warrior and Hilltroll Dark Knight in Mount Zhayolm.
    • Auction House: Materials > Smithing.
  • Check the Acid-Eaten Door for a cutscene.
  • Trade 3 slabs of Plumbago to the Acid-Eaten Door to receive a Lightning Cell.
  • Receive a cutscene at the lamp in Alzadaal Undersea Ruins.
  • With the Lightning Cell, enter the BCNM with Alexander at the Nyzul Isle assault door.
    • Buffs and TP are lost upon entering.
  • After winning the fight, return to Naja Salaheem with the Whisper of radiance to claim your reward.
    • If you lose the fight, you must collect 3 more slabs of Plumbago and receive a new Lightning Cell.

Side Note[]

  • It is possible to complete this quest with members who are on the Waking the Colossus quest that precedes this quest.

Friends To Help You[]

If you want someone to help you with this fight they MUST have the key item(s) as well to enter, doing so without it will result in you going in without them and the loss of your key item(s).
