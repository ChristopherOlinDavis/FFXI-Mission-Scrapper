Walkthrough[]

  • Head to Sauromugue Champaign (S) and examine the Bulwark Gate (F-6) for a cutscene.
    • The gate is the where you would zone into Port Jeuno from Sauromugue Champaign in current time.
    • Using the Campaign Arbiter or warping to Sauromugue Champaign via Voidwatch's Atmacite Refiner from Southern San d'Oria (S) would be the quickest way.
    • Otherwise, You can take the Survival Guide to present day Rolanberry Fields and taking the Cavernous Maw to go back in the past.
