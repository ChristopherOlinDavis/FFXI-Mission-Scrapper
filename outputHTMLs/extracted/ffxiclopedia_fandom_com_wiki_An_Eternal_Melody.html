Walkthrough[]

There are three cutscenes for this mission.

  1. Go to the Walnut Door on the top floor of the Tavnazian Safehold (K-7) for a cutscene, you will have the "Mysterious Amulet" Key Item returned to you.
  2. Speak with Justinius at J-6 to learn of where Ulmia has been.
  3. Take the southwest exit from the Tavnazian Safehold at H-6. Head out to examine the Dilapidated Gate located at Misareaux Coast (I-11) for a cutscene.
  4. Return to the Tavnazian Safehold and walk towards the Bridge for a final cutscene.
