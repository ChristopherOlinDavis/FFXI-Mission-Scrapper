Quick Fame[]

  • If you cannot access a specific quest, repeat quests:
    • Fear of the Dark III to level Abyssea - La Theine fame.
    • The Soul of the Matter to level Abyssea - Konschtat fame.
    • Weapons, Not Worries to level Abyssea - Tahrongi fame.
    • A Ward to End All Wards to level Abyssea - Vunkerl fame.
    • Unidentified Research Object to level Abyssea - Misareaux fame.
    • Threadbare Tribulations to level Abyssea - Attohwa fame.
    • Classrooms Without Borders to level Abyssea - Altepa fame.
    • Her Father's Legacy to level Abyssea - Grauberg fame.
    • Frozen Flame Redux to level Abyssea - Uleguerand fame.
  • See Quick Reputation guide for alternative options to level fame.
  • Fame can be checked at NPCs:
    • Namonutice (A) (E-3) Abyssea - La Theine.
    • Flaco (A) (I-13) Abyssea - Konschtat.
    • Zabirego-Hajigo (A) (H-12) Abyssea - Tahrongi.
    • Gulemont (A) (F-4) Abyssea - Vunkerl.
    • Izabele (A) (K-7) Abyssea - Misareaux.
    • Mendi (A) (G-10) Abyssea - Attohwa.
    • Honoi-Gomoi (A) (K-5) Abyssea - Altepa.
    • Parnika (A) (D-14) Abyssea - Grauberg.
    • Magriffon (A) (G-11) Abyssea - Uleguerand.
