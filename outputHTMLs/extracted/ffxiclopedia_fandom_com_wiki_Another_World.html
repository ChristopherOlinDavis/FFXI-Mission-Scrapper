Walkthrough[]

  • Zone into Southern San d'Oria from East Ronfaure for a cutscene (Home Point warp does not work).
    • Home Point #2 is closest to the East Ronfaure exit.
  • Head to Chateau d'Oraguille and speak with Halver (I-9) for a cutscene.
