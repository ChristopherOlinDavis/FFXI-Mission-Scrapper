Walkthrough[]

  • After completing all 3 Promyvions, you will be teleported to Lufaise Meadows and a cutscene will start. Don't teleport out or you will have a hard time coming back. Finish this quest first!
  • After the cutscene, proceed west to the Tavnazian Safehold at (E-8) or (F-10).
    • If you are under level ~50, be careful of aggro from mobs in Lufaise Meadows. In particular, Orcs and Gigas will aggro to sight as well as link, and Bugards will aggro to sound.
    • Quick Note: you CAN mount in this zone.
  • Once you zone into the Tavnazian Safehold, another cutscene will begin.
    • After the cutscene, don't forget to access the Survival Guide and the Home Point at the front of the safehold.
    • Also, if you already started VW content, you can talk to Owain to receive Hyacinth Stratum Abyssite, that allow you to start VW Tavnazia path, as well gives you access to new zones through VW teleportation.
