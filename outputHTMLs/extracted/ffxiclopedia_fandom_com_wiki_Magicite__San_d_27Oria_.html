  • Recommended: Level 60+ character, or level 50+ character with Sneak/Invisible, or party of 40+. Map of Beadeaux, Map of Davoi, and Map of Castle Oztroja.
  • If you already have the 4 key items (Crest of Davoi, Yagudo Torch, Coruscant rosary, Black Matinee Necklace) from doing this mission for another nation, you can be just about any level, as long as you have Sneak (Silent Oil) and Invisible (Prism Powder).
  • Unlike most missions, this mission is not offered by a Gate Guard.
  • This mission requires a certain level of Rank Bar. Complete 2 or 3 repeatable missions or trade 4 crystals to a Conquest Overseer.
    • Morlepiche at Ru'Lude Gardens (H-10) will accept crystals.

Walkthrough[]

A. Jeuno[]

  1. Talk to your embassy's receptionist in Ru'Lude Gardens to begin the mission.
  2. Check your ambassador's office door. You will receive the Archducal audience permit.
  3. Check the Audience Chamber (H-6). You will receive the Letter to Aldo.
  4. Talk to Aldo (J-8) in Lower Jeuno, inside Neptune's Spire Inn. You will receive a Silver bell.
    • To gain access to the Tenshodo HQ, obtain a Tenshodo Member's Card from the quest Tenshodo Membership or buy a Tenshodo Invite on the Auction House under Others > Misc and trade it to Ghebi Damomohe.
    • If currently on the Chains of Promathia mission A Vessel Without a Captain, its scene will appear first when interacting with the door. Talking to Aldo twice will be required after the cut-scene to prompt this mission's cut-scene.

Preliminary key items required to complete the mission

  • Crest of Davoi, Yagudo Torch, Coruscant rosary, Black Matinee Necklace.
    • The key items may be obtained in any order.
    • If you already have the key items, skip to the section labeled Magicite.
    • With Poison Potions and a friend who has Tractor and all of the key items, the only key item that is strictly necessary is Yagudo Torch.
      • If you wish to only obtain the Yagudo Torch, all you must do is speak to Paya-Sabya (I-8) in Upper Jeuno, then Muckvix (H-9) in Lower Jeuno.
  • These items are obtained by trading for them in Jeuno with trade-in items you must obtain (mostly from Beadeaux).

B. Preliminary Trade-ins (Beadeaux): Quadav Charm, Quadav Augury Shell[]

This section is to obtain the items needed for the Beadeaux key items. You can obtain the Yagudo Torch & Crest of Davoi in Jeuno first and get the Davoi/Oztroja magicite without going to Beadeaux until Davoi/Oztroja are done, if you want to do that for whatever reason.

  1. Travel to Beadeaux (entrance at L-11 of Pashhow Marshlands)
    • The fastest way is to book warp by Survival Guide to Derfland > Beadeaux.
    • Otherwise, Unity warp to Pashhow Marshlands (Level 122 category) and head to (L-11).
    • Quadav on the path inside go up to level 39 and will hearing-aggro to players under level 55 unless they are Sneaked.
  2. Follow the north/left wall to go down into Map 2 via exit A at (H-7).
  3. Go down following the north/right wall to examine The Mute at (G-7) and receive Silence (status effect).
    • This will allow you to pass by The Afflictor without being Cursed.
  4. Continue further in Map 2 to go up to Map 1 via exit B at (F-8).
  5. Go south following the west/left wall to find the ramp out of the water to the elevator floor at (E-10).
    1. Along the way use The Mute again if you ran out of silence (Afflictor coming up again soon after).
  6. Travel the elevated path at (I-9) to find and defeat De'Vyu Headhunter. It drops up to 4 Quadav Charms (only need one per player).
  7. Also on the elevated portion, find and defeat Go'Bhu Gascon at (F-6). It drops up to 4 Quadav Augury Shells (only need one per player).
  8. Coeurl Meat - drops from all Coeurls.
    • There are Coeurl in Meriphataud Mountains.
    • It can also be bought from the Auction House.

C. Jeuno - Trading trade-ins for the preliminary key items[]

You need 4 key items in total before leaving Jeuno to obtain all of the magicites at once.

  1. Crest of Davoi - Talk to Baudin at the southwest corner of Upper Jeuno (G-7) (boy on short steps).
    • Trade him the Coeurl Meat for a cutscene to obtain the Crest of Davoi.
  2. Yagudo Torch - Talk to Paya-Sabya (I-8) to initiate a cut scene.
    • Talk to Muckvix in Lower Jeuno (H-9) (Muckvix’s Junk Shop) to receive the Yagudo Torch.
  3. Beadeaux Key Items x2 - Talk to Sattal-Mansal at (J-8) (outside Aldo's room in the basement of Neptune's Spire).
    • Trade him the Quadav Charm to obtain the Coruscant rosary.
      1. This completes the quest Mysteries of Beadeaux I.
    • Trade him the Quadav Augury Shell to obtain the Black Matinee Necklace.
      • This completes the quest Mysteries of Beadeaux II.

D. Magicite (Davoi, Beadeaux, Oztroja)[]

  • The three magicite can be examined in any order.
    • If you are on the Windurst (S) Mission Howl from the Heavens, you will need to touch each Magicite twice: once for the shard, then once for the cutscene and magicite.

a. Davoi Magicite: Optistone[]

  1. Travel to Davoi (entrance at (G-12) of Jugner Forest).
    • Fastest way: Book warp by Survival Guide to Norvallen > Davoi.
    • Otherwise, Unity warp to Jugner Forest (Level 122 category) and head to (G-12).
  2. Go in and head west/north to go across a bridge and find the Wall of Dark Arts at (G-7) to get through it.
    • To pass through the Wall, clicking it opens up a UI menu.
      • You have to not be Invisible to click, and Orc will aggro and interrupt this.
    • Past the wall is entrance to #3 to the Monastic Cavern.
    • Orcs along the path go up to level 39 and will sight-aggro to players under level 55 unless they are Invisible.
    • Wolf Bats are level 25-29 and will hearing-aggro players under level 43 unless they are Sneaked.
    • A friend with the Crest of Davoi can Tractor you through the Wall.
  3. Check the Magicite at the end of the hall at (F-6) to receive Magicite: Optistone.

b. Beadeaux Magicite: Aurastone[]

  1. Travel to Beadeaux (entrance at L-11 of Pashhow Marshlands).
    • The fastest way is to book warp by Survival Guide to Derfland > Beadeaux.
    • Otherwise, Unity warp to Pashhow Marshlands (Level 122 category) and head to (L-11).
  2. Follow the north/left wall to go down into Map 2 via exit A at (H-7).
    • Quadav along the path go up to level 28 and will hearing-aggro to players under level 42 unless they are Sneaked.
  3. Go down and turn left to enter Qulun Dome (entrance #1) at (I-7).
  4. Open the door and proceed into the hall to (E-7).
    • If you get the message "The Silver bell, Coruscant rosary, Black Matinee Necklace, glow faintly," then move closer and try again.
    • A friend with the Silver bell, Coruscant rosary, and Black Matinee Necklace can Tractor you through the door.
    • As there are no mobs in Qulun Dome, you will need to use Poison Potions to kill yourself first.
  5. Check the Magicite at the end of the hall at (F-8) to receive Magicite: Aurastone.

c. Castle Oztroja Magicite: Orastone[]

  1. Travel to Castle Oztroja (entrance at (L-8) in Meriphataud Mountains).
    • The fastest way is to book warp by Survival Guide to Aragoneu > Castle Oztroja.
    • Yagudo on the path inside are up to level 49 and will sight-aggro to players under level 68 unlessthey are Invisible.
    • Bats will also aggro unless players are Sneaked.
  2. Go through the Brass Door (I-8) and go up the stairs to Map 3 (A) at (J-8).
    • One lever opens the door, and the other triggers the trapdoor.
    • You will not be able to throw a lever unless you are standing on the trapdoor.
    • The correct lever changes every Vana'diel day.
    • Since there is a significant delay, an easy way to avoid the trap is to throw a lever and then immediately run off the trapdoor.
  3. After (A), go north then west to go up to Map 7 via (F) at (G-7).
  4. From (F), go north to enter Map 2 via (I) at (I-7).
  5. Going forward, you can hug the right/West wall to reach the Yagudo Torch at (H-9).
    • Click on the Yagudo Torch to light them, which opens the Brass Door.
      • You have to not be Invisible to click, and Yagudo will aggro and interrupt this.
  6. Go through the door and go right/West to enter the Altar Room (entrance #1) at (G-10).
    • If you spoke to Paya-Sabya and Muckvix, you will get a cutscene with Fickblix. If you are repeating this mission and you skipped those two, this cutscene will be skipped.
  7. Check the Magicite at the end of the hall at (G-8) to receive Magicite: Orastone.

E. Return to Jeuno[]

  1. Return to the Audience Chamber at Ru'Lude Gardens (H-6). You will receive an Airship pass.
    • If you already have an Airship pass, you will receive 20,000 gil instead.
  2. Return to your embassy's receptionist to complete the mission. You will receive Rank 5, Message to Jeuno, and 10,000 gil.
    • Mission 5-1 will begin immediately. You will not be able to change allegiance until you complete 5- 1 or reject the mission offer in the following cutscene.

Game Description

Mission Orders
Seize the three types of magicite from Davoi, Beadeaux, and Castle Oztroja to prevent the Shadow Lord's return.
