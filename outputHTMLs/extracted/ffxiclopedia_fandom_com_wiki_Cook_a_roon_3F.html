Walkthrough[]

  • Ququroon wants the following fish:
    • Istavrit
    • Istakoz
    • Mercanbaligi
    • Ahtapot
    • Istiridye
  • If you are unable to fish up these fish, then they are only available from the Auction House, or possibly the Fishing Guild Merchant, Wahnid.
Note that the availability of goods from the Auction House depends on whether players have put them up for sale or not; and similarly, the availability of goods from Guild Merchants depends on whether players have sold them to those merchants or not.
  • You can complete this quest easily at level 32 fishing skill if you have a Lu Shang's Fishing Rod.
    • Fishing in Nashmau using a Minnow, Sinking Minnow and Crayfish Balls, you will eventually get 4/5 fish.
    • For the last one (Istavrit) you'll need to travel to Talacca Cove and fish using a Sinking Minnow.
      • Or, you can catch 4/5 of the fish in Talacca Cove, and the Istiridye can be caught in Nashmau.
        • Or, you can also purchase it an (instavrit) from the Green Thumb Moogle after obtaining the Blue sea monk memento by Monster Rearing/Blue Sea Monk with inside the Mog Garden's.
Keep sneak up, you can fish up some very nasty monsters in the zone. As long as you release any bites with the monster message "Something clamps onto your line ferociously!", you needn't worry about Sneak.
  • There is a possibility that Ququroon will fail to make the Nashmau Stew when you bring him the fish.
The quest is still considered completed, but you will not receive the reward.
What triggers Ququroon's failure is not known.
  • Rat Race is tied to this quest, unknown if this one is a prerequisite, or if it must also be active to get the Nashmau Stew reward.

Game Description[]

Client
Ququroon (North of the Weapon Shop, Nashmau)
Summary
Ququroon wants to practice cooking so he can open a restaurant one day. Help him out by bringing him the ingredients he requires.
