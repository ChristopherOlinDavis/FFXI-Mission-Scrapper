Walkthrough[]

  • This mission requires a certain level of Rank Bar. Complete Wading Beasts 8-10 times or trade 4 crystals to a Conquest Overseer.
  • Talk to a Bastok Gate Guard to receive the mission.
  • Go to Bastok Mines and talk to Medicine Eagle at (H-5) for a cutscene.
  • You must then go talk to Drake Fang who is at (H-6) in Zeruhn Mines next to the boat from Palborough Mines. (You dont have to get on the boat to talk to him, just come through Zeruhn). You will see a cutscene where Iron Eater will approve your mission and tell you to go check out Altepa.
  • Go to the center of Western Altepa Desert (Revelation Rock). Southwest of the rock is a ??? at (G-8). Examine it, and the Western Sphinx and Eastern Sphinx will pop.
    • A quick way to get there is to Unity warp (Level 125 category).
  • Kill one or both of the Western Sphinx and Eastern Sphinx.
    • Soloable with Trust at level 65. Trivial at Item Level. (see testimonials).
    • Only Western Sphinx or Eastern Sphinx must be killed, but you cannot obtain the key item until the other is either dead or de-aggroed.
    • If you fail to kill the NM(s) wait about 10 to 15 minutes and you will be able to trigger the fight again.
    • If someone else aggros the 2nd Sphinx, you cannot obtain the Altepa moonpebble. Confirmed for aggro + depop, have to check if someone else claims and kills it.
  • After killing the Sphinxes, check the ??? again to get an Altepa moonpebble.
  • Talk to Tall Mountain downstairs (J-7) in Bastok Mines on Ore Street by the "Restricted Section" in order to complete this mission.
    • The NPC is next to Bastok Mines Home Point #3.
