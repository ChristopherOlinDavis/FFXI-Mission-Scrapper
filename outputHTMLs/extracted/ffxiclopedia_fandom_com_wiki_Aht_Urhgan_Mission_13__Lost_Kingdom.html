Walkthrough[]

  • Check Jazaraat's Headstone (E-10) in Caedarva Mire for a short cutscene. This can be reached from the western exit of Nashmau.
  • Check the headstone again to spawn the NM Jazaraat.
  • After Jazaraat is defeated, check the headstone again for another cutscene and the Ephramadian Gold Coin

Game Description[]

Mission Orders
Pyopyoroon of Nashmau has advised you to head west to a place with many "stooones" to find a special "ghooost." Perhaps it will lead you to the ghost ship...
