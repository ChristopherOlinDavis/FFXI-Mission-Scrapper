Walkthrough[]

  • After receiving the cutscene from the previous mission, zone into Mhaura or Selbina for a cutscene.
  • Note: The path you choose will change what you have to do next. Once you zone into either area, it will not be possible to flag the mission in the other one.
    • Selbina Path:
      • Go to the Mayor's Residence and talk to Naillina (F-9) Choose the first option from the dialogue; "Did you want an adventurer?" This will trigger a cutscene in which Abelard asks you to bring 3 clumps of Bee Pollen, which is dropped by Huge Wasps in Konschtat Highlands and La Theine Plateau, or can be bought in the Auction House under Miscellaneous Items. This will trigger the next mission in the storyline, Set Free.
    • Mhaura Path:
      • Go to the Governor's Residence and talk to Numi Adaligo (F-9). Choose the first option from the dialogue; "You're searching for an adventurer?" This will trigger a cutscene in which Ekokoko will ask you to bring 3 Mandragora Dewdrops, which are dropped by Pygmaiois in Tahrongi Canyon, or can be bought in the Auction House under Miscellaneous Items. This will trigger the next mission, Set Free.
