Walkthrough[]

  • Speak with Camille in Wajaom Woodlands (M-7) for a cutscene.
    • Use the Whitegate exit under the south stairs at (H-10).
  • Choose to participate in Voidwatch operations to receive Amber Stratum Abyssite.
  • Defeat the 4 monsters:
    • Dimgruzub in Arrapago Reef, Map 1 (I-10), (G-8), or (G-10)
      • Atmacite Refiner teleports you to Caedarva Mire (I-6) for entrance to map 1.
    • Brekekekex in Caedarva Mire (I-11), (I-9) or (F-11)
      • Atmacite Refiner teleports you to Caedarva Mire (H-10) next to Nashmau.
    • Yalungur in Mamook (J-8), (L-8) or (?-?)
      • Atmacite Refiner teleports you to Wajaom Woodlands (E-12) for entrance to map 1.
    • Vanasarvik in Mount Zhayolm (M-6), (J-8), or (L-8)
      • Atmacite Refiner teleports you to Mount Zhayolm (L-7).
  • Return to Camille to finish this quest, upgrade to Amber Stratum Abyssite II, and automatically begin the next quest.

Game Description[]

Client
Camille (Wajaom Woodlands)
Summary
Planar rifts have been reported in Arrapago Reef, Caedarva Mire, Mamook and Mount Zhayolm. Engage the Voidwalkers at each location and obliterate them!
