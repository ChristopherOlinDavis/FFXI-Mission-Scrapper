Walkthrough[]

  • Visit Aydeewa Subterrane, Map 3 (G-10).
    • The easiest way to get to Map 3 (G-10) is to enter Aydeewa Subterrane from Wajaom Woodlands (E-10) (marked as entrance 8 on the map).
      • Once inside, follow the path south until you come to a ledge with some worms on the bottom. Drop and keep heading south. Take the first turn west and keep heading west to the ???. (Note: The ledge you drop off is at H-9, head West for only a short time and take the first path South - if you see a room with tigers then you're in the right place. Keep right as much as possible to help avoid aggro.)
  • Run through into the middle of the room and approach the stairs ruins (G-10) for a very short cutscene with NPC Wawaroon.
    • The cutscene will start before you reach the base of the stairs.
    • The cutscene WILL drop your Invisible effect, the moment the cutscene ends the level 64-68 chigoes nearby might aggro you. Recommend Blink and Stoneskin (if possible) before entering the room with the stairs if that represents a threat to the player.
  • Head to Nashmau and talk to Tsetseroon again for a cutscene which flags the quest.
    • Trade him a Tin Ore and Cobalt Jellyfish to receive the Pot of Tsetseroon's Stew.
  • Go back to Aydeewa Subterrane, Map 3 (G-10) and check the ??? on the stairs for another cutscene with NPC Wawaroon.
    • NOTE: If you stand on the steps above the ??? before you drop Invisible, there's a good chance you won't aggro any chigoes.
  • After cutscene, you are given two options to examine Wawaroon's bag:
    • If you choose "I am no thief", you get nothing.
    • If you choose "Yes", you obtain the Map of Aydeewa Subterrane immediately. (Best Choice)
  • Return back to Nashmau and talk to Tsetseroon again for a final cutscene and reward.
    • If you chose "I am no thief" earlier, you obtain the Map of Aydeewa Subterrane.
    • If you chose "Yes" earlier, you obtain an Imperial Bronze Piece.

Game Description[]

Client
Tsetseroon (Junk Seller, Nashmau)

Tsetseroon needs a cobalt jellyfish and a chunk of tin ore to make his special stew.
