Walkthrough[]

  • You must zone after completing the previous quest in order to get this quest.
  • Talk to Iruki-Waraki at (K-9) in Aht Urhgan Whitegate (second floor of the Way of the Devout) for a cutscene.
  • You can be on any job from here on.
  • Trade Iruki-Waraki a Sleeping Potion and a Chai for a cutscene.
    • Sleeping Potion can be bought from the auction house and Chai from the Shararat Teahouse (K/J-12) in southern Whitegate.
    • You can have the potion and tea before you start the quest and just trade to him after the first cutscene.
  • Travel to Nashmau and speak with Dnegan at (H-6) on the second floor for a cutscene. During the cutscene you will be asked three questions.
    • On the first question choose either "Ask how to get around Nashmau" or "Shall we go out for tea?".
      • Selecting "Ask about the automaton" ends the cutscene and you must return another game day to complete the quest.
    • On the second question, choosing either Chai or Coffee will work.
      • If you chose "I'll pass, thanks.", Yafahb also declines to order and you must wait to try again. Just talk with Dnegan again the next game day to restart the quest.
    • On the third question, "the cup with a suspicious VAPOR" is the correct choice.
  • Exit Nashmau by the north gate to enter Caedarva Mire. Get the Lamian Fang Key at (I-7) on a small ledge behind a Jnun (can be obtained once per game day, only one needed for whole party) and enter Arrapago Reef at (I-6).
  • Run to the Iron Gate at (J-10) and open it up with the Lamian Fang Key and continue running directly north through the area to (H-7). Open the door there and go through. From there, go north and find the tunnel entrance at (I-6). Go east and then take the fork going south in the tunnel back to Caedarva Mire.
  • Shortly after you exit, click on the ??? for a cutscene.
    • Do not drop down or you must walk through Arrapago Reef again.
  • After the cutscene, Iruki-Waraki rewards you.
