Walkthrough[]

  • Aht Urhgan Whitegate:
  • Talk to Mishhar (H-8), 2nd floor for first cutscene. (note: you must wait until after JST midnight to start this quest after completing Give Peace a Chance)
  • Run into the Tea House at (J-12) for the second cutscene.
  • Talk to Mishhar again for a third cutscene.
  • Enter Mamook from Wajaom Woodlands D-12 (or via a Survival Guide to get exactly there) : Examine the ??? around the J-7 Area right next to the zone.
  • Jade Sepulcher: Examine the Ornamental Door for a cutscene.
    • Buffs do NOT wear upon entering battlefield.
    • Examining the Ornamental Door again will allow access to the BCNM fight. Very important: none of your friends can access the BCNM if they don't have this quest started or finished yet.
      • You enter a BCNM against three Mamool Ja:
        • Shadowhand Kajeel Ja - NIN
        • Whitetattoo Rahool Ja - WHM
        • Blacktattoo Vedoll Ja - BLM
          • Note: They all can resist an Elemental Seal Sleepga and Sleep II from a RDM75, and are also highly resistant to Silence.
    • Three NPC Humes will spawn behind the Mamool Ja, Wabjahl, Jasweem, Tiyaash, they will attack the Mamool Ja and run around in circles. The battle will end if all of them are defeated. Therefore it's difficult to solo the BC unless you have very high damage output and can survive the BLM's nukes.
    • You cannot cure them, and the mobs will often focus their attacks on them, regardless of hate.
    • The monsters are extremely weak to a level 75, easily duoed by a mage and melee.
  • Return to Mishhar for your reward.
    • The reward given is based on the main job when talking with Mishhar after completing the BC.
