Walkthrough[]

Rhapsody Effects[]

Experience Gains

  • 30% bonus to experience and limit point gains

Unity Concord and Records of Eminence

  • 30% bonus to Unity accolade gains

Battle Content

  • Voidwatch:
    • Reduces the amount of time that must pass after using /fume before a monster’s weakpoint may be changed again to thirty seconds
    • Reduces the price of Phase Displacer to 1,000 gil
    • Reduces the amount of Cruor required to enhance Atmacite to 1/20 the original value
  • Legion:
    • Reduces the price of Legion tome page: minimus to 1,000 gil and the price of Legion tome page: maximus to 2,000 gil
  • Abyssea:
    • The following key items will always change color: Clear Demilune Abyssite / Colorful Demilune Abyssite / Scarlet Demilune Abyssite / Azure Demilune Abyssite / Viridian Demilune Abyssite / Jade Demilune Abyssite / Sapphire Demilune Abyssite / Crimson Demilune Abyssite / Emerald Demilune Abyssite / Vermillion Demilune Abyssite / Indigo Demilune Abyssite
    • Increases the amount of time that may be spent in Abyssea per Traverser stone to sixty minutes (Earth time)
  • Limbus:
    • Reduces the amount of time between purchases of Cosmo-Cleanse to one hour (Earth time)
    • Reduces the price of Cosmo-Cleanse to 1,000 gil
  • Campaign:
    • Reduces the amount of time between issuance of Op Credit to ten minutes (Earth time)
    • Reduces the amount of time between Campaign Evaluations to one hour (Earth time)
  • Meeble Burrows: Reduces the amount of time between replenishment of Moblin pheromone sack to ten minutes (Earth time)
  • Moblin Maze Mongers:
    • Reduces the time that must pass before entering a new maze to one hour (Earth time)
    • Increases the frequency of accumulating Cave Conservation Points to one every twenty-four minutes

Vendors

  • Additional items for sale by Curio Vendor Moogles located in the three nations

Game Description

Mission Orders
Though victorious over the ambassador, you were banished to a land without time where you met your future self. Is Vana'diel working with you or against you? Where is Iroha? Rendezvous with your allies in Norg.
