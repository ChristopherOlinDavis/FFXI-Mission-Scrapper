Walkthrough[]

  • Head to Ru'Lude Gardens (Home Point #1) and talk to Pherimociel at (G-6) for a cutscene.
    • This may yet again need a few tries for the correct dialogue. The mission dialog begins "You adventurers are quick to hear the news!".
  • Check the Audience Chamber door upstairs at (H-6) for a cutscene with Prishe, Ulmia, Esha'ntarl and Louverance (you may have to click the door twice if you participate in Voidwatch).
  • Head to Selbina and talk to Mathilde at (H-9), inside of the Weavers' Guild (inside the door closest to the Home Point, near the sheep pen), for a cutscene.
  • Optional: Talk to Mathilde again for more dialogue.
