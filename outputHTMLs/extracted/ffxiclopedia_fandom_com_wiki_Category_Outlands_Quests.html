Quick Fame[]

  • If you cannot access a specific quest, repeat quests:
    • Only the Best to level Rabao fame.
    • Mihgo's Amigo to level Norg fame.
    • Mandragora-Mad to level Kazham fame.
  • See Quick Reputation Guide for alternative options to level fame.
  • Fame can be checked at NPCs:
    • Waylea (G-9) Rabao.
    • Vaultimand (H-8) Norg.
    • Ney Hiparujah (I-11) Kazham.
