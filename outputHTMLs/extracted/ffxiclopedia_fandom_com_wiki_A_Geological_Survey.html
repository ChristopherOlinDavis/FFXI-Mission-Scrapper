Walkthrough[]

  1. Talk to a Bastok Gate Guard to receive the mission.
  2. (Metalworks) Talk to Cid (H-8), inside Cid's Lab, for a Blue Acidity Tester.
  3. (South Gustaberg) Go to (D-9) the Dangrud Wadi entrance.
  4. (Dangruf Wadi) Go to geyser at (I-8) and stand on the geyser to jump up the ledge.
    • Just hug the right/North wall after entering, it's a short walk and you'll know when you reach the ledge.
    • This will change the Blue Acidity Tester to the Red Acidity Tester.
      • No message will be displayed in the log to inform you of this; you may wish to check your Temporary Key Items to verify the change before leaving the area.
    • There are 2 Goblins in the geyser area, and 1 right before the geyser area.
      • They are level 5-8 and will sight-Aggro to players under level 15 unless they are Invisible.
      • The geyser can take minutes to spout you up, so be ready to fight or have invisible.
      • You can purchase Circumspection from the Grounds Tome by the entrance for 5 tabs.
  5. (Metalworks) Return to Cid to complete the mission.

Game Description

Mission Orders
Assist Chief Engineer Cid in conducting an analysis of the gaseous emissions in the Dangruf Wadi. Meet with Cid for a more detailed explanation.
