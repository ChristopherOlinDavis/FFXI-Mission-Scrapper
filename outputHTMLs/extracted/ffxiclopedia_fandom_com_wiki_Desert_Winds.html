Walkthrough[]

  • Objective: Zone into Aht Urhgan Whitegate for a cutscene.
    • Option 1: The fastest way to get the cutscene for this mission (and be in place for the next mission) is by arriving via Aht Urhgan Whitegate Home Point #2.
    • Option 2: For a new character, the fastest way is to take the UNM Wanted Battle (Lv125) warp (cost: 100 accolades) via the Unity Concord NPC (one of the NPCs next to the Sparks NPC) to Wajaom Woodlands.
      • Then enter Whitegate via Wajaom Woodlands (M-7) by clicking on the door of the building there. (This step is not necessary if you arrive via the Home Point.)
