Walkthrough[]

Stoneserpent General Zazarg must be in the Commoner's Ward/Al Zahbi (H-6) for this quest to be accessible or completable. If he is being held prisoner talking to Talhaal will not lead to a cutscene.

  • Talk to Talhaal in Al Zahbi at (H-6). He will tell you to meet him at the Shararat Teahouse later.
  • Head back the Shararat Teahouse in Aht Urhgan Whitegate and talk to Fari-Wari again. Talhaal will show up to tell you the story of Zazarg.
    • (This cutscene will run at least 3m:30s.)
  • For the rest of his story he tells you to find the general himself.
  • Obtain a Rusty Medal from a Wajaom Tiger in Wajaom Woodlands or Bhaflau Thickets.
    • The Rusty Medal may also be purchased on the Auction House under: Others > Misc.
  • Trade it to the Leypoint in Wajaom Woodlands at (G-8) for the final cutscene.
    • (This cutscene is at least 5m:55s long.)

Trust: Zazarg[]

  • Speak with Fari-Wari after completing the quest to learn Trust: Zazarg.
    • This requires Trust: Bastok, Trust: San d'Oria, or Trust: Windurst to be completed first.
    • Zazarg's alter ego cannot be acquired if he is currently held prisoner by beastman forces in Besieged.
    • Zazarg makes a reference to your membership in the Seventh Cohors if you have completed the quest The Fighting Fourth.
