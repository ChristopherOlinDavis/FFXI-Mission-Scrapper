Walkthrough[]

This quest is the final of multiple quests involved in the upgrade of Mythic Weapons.

  • Return to Caedarva Mire's cemetery at (E-10) and examine it with the mythic weapon equipped for a cutscene with the Balrahn Eidolon.
  • Trade the Mythic Weapon to the gravestone to fully unlock it.
    • To upgrade your Mythic Weapon to higher levels and eventually Item Level 119, Magian Trials are required.
  • After receiving the unlocked weapon check the Imperial Whitegate with no weapons equipped for another cutscene with Ovjang, Mnejing, Nashmeira, Raubahn, and Bharifhal.
  • Walk up the first ramp inside Salaheem's Sentinels for a cutscene with Abquhbah and Naja Salaheem and a reward of 3 Imperial Silver Pieces.

Notes[]

  • When viewing the cutscenes for this quest at a Melody Minstrel or Goblin Footprint, the mythic weapon discussed will be selected randomly.
