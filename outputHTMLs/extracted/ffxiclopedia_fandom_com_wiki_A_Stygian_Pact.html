Walkthrough[]

  • This quest is flagged and completed in the same way as The Rider Cometh and Unwavering Resolve, and has the same rewards for completion. The only difference from previous completions is new, unchanging dialogue from Odin, Nashmeira, Ovjang and Mnejing after completion, and the removal of the dialogue selection where you may ask for the release of Odin's knight, among other boons.

Participating in the Fight[]

  • To help on this fight, players must be on either The Rider Cometh, Unwavering Resolve, or A Stygian Pact and possess a Talisman key and Spatial Pressure Barometer. Failure to meet these requirements will cause these party members to be left behind while the others' key items will be lost as they enter the battlefield.
