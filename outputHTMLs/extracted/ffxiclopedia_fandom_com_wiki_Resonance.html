Walkthrough[]

  • Zone into any area connecting to a Mog House in San d'Oria, Windurst, or Bastok for a cutscene.
  • After the cutscene, zone into Mhaura or Selbina for another cutscene that will trigger the next mission.

If you did not start this mission prior to the July 2023 Version Update you are given an option to skip the opening cutscene. If you do so, you may start this mission from the Tales' Beginning at any of the following location:

  • Southern San d'Oria (H-8)
  • Northern San d'Oria (J-10)
  • Port San d'Oria (H-9)
  • Bastok Mines (I-9)
  • Bastok Markets (I-8)
  • Port Bastok (D-7)
  • Windurst Waters (F-5)
  • Windurst Walls (C-6)
  • Port Windurst (B-5)
  • Windurst Woods (J-10)
