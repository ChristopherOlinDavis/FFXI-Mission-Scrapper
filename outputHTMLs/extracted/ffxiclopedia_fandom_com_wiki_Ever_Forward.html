Walkthrough[]

  • Examine the Imperial Whitegate at Aht Urhgan Whitegate L-8 & L-9 for a cutscene.
    • If you have already completed all Treasures of Aht Urhgan Missions through ToAU 46, you will receive a cutscene with Arzizah, Nashmeira, Mnejing, and Ovjang, after which you will proceed to The Endless Sky and then Aphmau's Light and finally Reunited.
    • If you haven't completed all Treasures of Aht Urhgan Missions, do the missions until you flag Aht Urhgan Mission 12: Royal Puppeteer after which you can proceed to examine the Whitegate and then continue to Reunited.
      • NOTE: Numerous Aht Urhgan missions lock you from finishing this mission. Consult this list. Once you get this cutscene, it's strongly recommended you finish up to Uncertain Destinations before continuing Treasures of Aht Urhgan.
      • If you have only just started Treasures of Aht Urhgan, it will take approximately 1.5 to 2 hours to get up to mission 12, most of it being cutscenes with only one long travel section.
      • Some users report that despite having completed the Aht Urhgan Missions, no cut scene triggered when returning to their home nation. Tested with all home nations.
