Quick Fame[]

  • Fame can be checked at NPC Iyvah Halohm (G-8) Eastern Adoulin.
  • The chain quests It Sets My Heart Aflutter, A Good Pair of Crocs, and A Shot in the Dark can be done repeatedly to increase fame. Once A Shot in the Dark is complete, you can start over with It Sets My Heart Aflutter.
