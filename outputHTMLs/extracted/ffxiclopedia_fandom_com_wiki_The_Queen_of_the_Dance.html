Walkthrough[]

  • Check the Lion Springs Tavern door (K-6) for another cutscene.
    • You cannot see the cutscene while the city is under attack, although changes to Campaign made it so that city attacks seemingly cannot happen at all.
  • Go to Upper Jeuno and talk to Turlough (G-7) for a cutscene and the Mayakov Show Ticket.
    • Turlough is watching the dancers.
    • In case you didnt start Lakeside Minuet quest (to unlock DNC), you can do it by having a job over lv30 and talking to Laila thats close by.
  • Return to Southern San d'Oria (S) and check the Lion Springs Tavern door for another cutscene.
