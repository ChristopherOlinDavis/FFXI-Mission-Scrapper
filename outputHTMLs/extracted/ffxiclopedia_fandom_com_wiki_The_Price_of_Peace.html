Notes[]

  • You are not eligible to skip missions by trading in crystals until you are rank 2 or higher.
  • At level 10, enemies in Giddeus will likely aggro but are Easy Prey. Be careful not to pull large groups, especially around the 2 Giddeus NPCs.
  • At level 13, you should be able to complete this mission only pulling minimal aggro.

Walkthrough[]

Windurst -> Sarutabaruta[]

  1. Accept the mission from any Windurst gate guard.
  2. {Windurst Waters J-9} Rhinostery - In the southern portion the zone (Home Point #3), talk to Leepe-Hoppe on the roof.
    • He will give you the Food Offering and Drink Offering.
      • OPTIONAL: Talk to Ohbiru-Dohbiru inside the Rhinostery.
  3. {West Sarutabaruta F-8} Enter Giddeus.
    • The Outpost in West Sarutabaruta (H-6) is near the Giddeus entrance.

Giddeus[]

  1. {Giddeus H-7} Follow the path and at all intersections take the left-hand choice until you reach (H-7).
    • Talk to the Yagudo NPC Laa Mozi (standing at the mouth of a cave entrance) to give Food Offering.
  2. {Giddeus G-7} Go back the way you came, still picking the left-hand choice, until you reach (G-7).
    • Talk to the Yagudo NPC Ghoo Pakya (also standing at the mouth of a cave entrance) to give Drink Offering.

{Windurst Waters J-9} Rhinostery[]

  1. Return to the roof of the Rhinostery for another cutscene.
  2. Afterwards, report back to the Windurst Gate Guard to complete the mission.
    • Even though the cutscene says to return to the Windurst Gate Guard where you started, any Windurst Gate Guard will do.

Game Description

Mission Orders
Receive two bags of food offerings from the Rhinostery, and deliver one bag to each of the Yagudo who stand by the altars of offerings in Giddeus. Don't forget to report back to the Rhinostery after completing the mission.
