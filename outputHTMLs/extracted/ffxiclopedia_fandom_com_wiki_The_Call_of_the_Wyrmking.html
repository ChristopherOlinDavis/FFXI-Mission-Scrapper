Walkthrough[]

  • Head to Port Bastok (Home Point #3) and approach the Departures Entrance to the Air Travel Agency at F-7 to receive a cutscene.
    • Note: If the cutscene does not activate, zone and try again.
  • Then go to the Metalworks and talk to Cid (H-8).
