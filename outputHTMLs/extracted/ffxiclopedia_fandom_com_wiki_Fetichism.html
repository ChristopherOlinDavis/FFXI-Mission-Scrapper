Walkthrough[]

  1. Receive the mission from a Bastok Gate Guard.
  2. Trade each of the following items to a guard to complete the mission.
    • Fetich Head | Fetich Arms | Fetich Torso | Fetich Legs
      • These items can be purchased from the Auction House. They drop from the following varieties of Quadav:
        • Amber, Brass, Greater, Old, Onyx, and Veteran.
      • Palborough Mines is a great place for farming due to the high density of Quadav; you need only ignore Amethyst, Copper, and Young Quadav.
        • Palborough Mines can be reached from North Gustaberg at (K-3). It can also be reached by the Home Point if you've been there before or teleporting to "Waughroon Shrine" via Domenic (J-7) in Lower Jeuno if you have completed Beyond Infinity.
      • The cutscene will differ notably depending on which Bastok Gate Guard you trade the items to. To view all of the cutscenes you will need four sets of items; however, as they are , you will need to farm each set one at a time.
  3. Optional: Talk to Gumbah (J-7) in an upper-level house in Bastok Mines for an extra cutscene.

Game Description

Mission Orders
Hunt the Quadav in the Palborough Mines and collect the four parts of a Quadav fetich.
