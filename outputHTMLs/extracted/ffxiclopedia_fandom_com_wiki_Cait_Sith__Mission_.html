Walkthrough[]

  • To flag this mission, after finishing your nation's 3 required quests, check any of the Jeuno-area maws, past or present (if present, it must have been activated in the past first), for a cutscene.
    • If you have already started the Rhapsodies of Vana'diel mission Cauterize, Cait Sith will hand you the Lightsworm required to advance to Ganged Up On at the end of this cutscene.
  • Complete the next round of Nation quests:
    • Bastok: Light in the Darkness, Burden of Suspicion, or
    • San d'Oria: Boy and the Beast, Wrath of the Griffon, or
    • Windurst: Knot Quite There, A Manifest Problem
  • Zone into Southern San d'Oria (S) from East Ronfaure (S) for a cutscene.
    • It does not matter what nation you are doing your quests for, you must go to Southern San d'Oria (S).
    • You will not get the cutscene by going to Southern San d'Oria (S) via Home Point or Survival Guide; you must walk in from East Ronfaure (S).

Game Description

Mission Orders
Beyond the cavernous maws lies the age of the Crystal War. You have agreed to aid Cait Sith in her attempt to lessen the pain inflicted in this time of strife.
