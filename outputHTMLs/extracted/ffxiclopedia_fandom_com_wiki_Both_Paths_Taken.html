Walkthrough[]

  • At the end of the previous cut scene, you'll be put in Empyreal Paradox. If you need to leave, you can do so via a nearby Dimensional Portal to return to a random Crag and come directly back to Empyreal Paradox via any crag's Dimensional Portal. While finishing the mission removes the Empyreal Paradox option from the Crag portal, this portal will continue to work.
    • If you are not on or past Dawn, you cannot zone to The Garden of Ru'Hmet through the transporter. No prompt will come up.
      • Casting Escape will allow you to zone into Al'Taieu, however you will not be able to zone from there into the Grand Palace of Hu'Xzoi, therefore you cannot obtain the home point there. Furthermore, unless you have some form of warp or teleport, the only way to get out of there would be to let a mob kill you.
    • If you dont have the home point for Ru'Hmet, but have Dawn done, now is a good time to get it.
  • Walk forward through a doorway and examine the Transcendental Radiance within the area to enter a battlefield against the Disjoined One, a Dark Knight utilizing Sword weapon skills.
    • Upon entry, Siren's plume loses its power and becomes Motionless plume. If you fail, you must restore its power by re-examining the dimensional portal you entered from and selecting Empyreal Paradox to reattempt the battle. Once you zone into the Empyreal Paradox you will be given a new Siren's Plume.
    • 15-minute time limit, 6-member limit.
    • Buffs wear on entry.
    • Trusts may be summoned after entry, before engaging.
      • Also, suposing you are with a weak party, you can enter the battlefield as a party, split once you enter then call trusts. Do notice, despite the party being separated, there is no restriction to claiming so all players, in party or no, can engage.
    • Assisted by Iroha, a Samurai/Scholar who casts Embrava on herself and the party and unleashes exclusive weapon skills, including Amatsu: Choun, Amatsu: Fuga, and Amatsu: Hanadoki.
      • Iroha can't be targeted with Enhancing Magic, but she is affected by Blood Pact: Wards.
  • A cutscene plays automatically after the battle and leaves you beside a random Crag's Dimensional Portal.
