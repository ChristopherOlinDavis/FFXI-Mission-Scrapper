Walkthrough[]

  • Note: After completing the Quest Divine Might, if you have not received your rare/ex earring, enter Shrine of Ru'Avitau from H-8/9 in Ru'Aun Gardens.
  • After completing ZM14 Ark Angels, go to Norg (Home Point #1) and talk to Gilgamesh (L-8).
  • Optional: Then go to Lower Jeuno (Home Point #2) and talk to Aldo in Tenshodo HQ J-8 for a cutscene.
  • Go back to Ru'Aun Gardens.
  • Enter Shrine of Ru'Avitau again for a cutscene.

Mission Orders
The door deep within the Shrine of Ru'Avitau is sealed with some form of supernatural power. Could this be the work of Eald'narche?
