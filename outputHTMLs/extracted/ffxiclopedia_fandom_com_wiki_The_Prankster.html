Walkthrough[]

  • Talk to Ahaadah (G-6) (upper level) for a cutscene.
  • Go to (I-11), down the ramp and by 2 baskets under a platform for the next cutscene. (Stand under the shelter on the east side of the dock, on the right of the two baskets as if you were a third basket.)
  • Go to (I-12), upper level, at the end of the walk for another cutscene.
  • Take the (H-7) exit to Bhaflau Thickets
  • Find an ??? in the bushes in (I-8), and, when ready, check it to spawn the Plague Chigoe (NM).
    • Caution! Plague Chigoe (NM) has an added effect of Aspir (1% of Max MP/hit) and is approximately a level 75 mob who hits hard and very, very fast. It seems to have a very high rate of Triple Attack, to a degree to seem like it has constant Hundred Fists.
      • Additional effect: MP drain is based on your maximum MP.
    • This NM is deceptively strong and can kill even level 99 players if not careful.
    • Hits for around 50 a hit
    • Has approximately 5000 HP.
  • Defeat it and check the ??? again for a cutscene and the Map of Caedarva Mire.
  • Note: You only have to kill it once for all party members.
  • The Plague Chigoe will not despawn immediately if you zone into Whitegate through the back entrance
