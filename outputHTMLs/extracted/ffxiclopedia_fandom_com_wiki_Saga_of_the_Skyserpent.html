Walkthrough[]

Skyserpent General Rughadjeen must be in the Commoner's Ward/Al Zahbi (G-7) for this quest to be accessible or completable. If he is being held prisoner talking to Fari-Wari will not lead to a cutscene.
  • Talk to Fari-Wari in Aht Urhgan Whitegate to start quest (K-12).
  • Enter Halvung from the (C-8) Wajaom Woodlands entrance.
    • The Wajaom Woodlands Survival Guide is at this point.
  • Go North to H-11, through the Cast Bronze Gate.
All monsters leading up to the gate detect by hearing, although there may be a single troll on the other side of the gate, which detects by sight.
  • There is a ??? on the ground, just to your left after you pass through the gate. Click it to obtain the Lilac Ribbon Key Item.
  • Return to Fari-Wari for a story of a Besieged battle against the Mamool Ja and the Trolls two years ago.
    • The cutscene lasts at least 4m:40s.
    • All times given for this line of quests depend on your connection speed and how quickly you proceed through the dialog.
  • Talk to Biyaada in Al Zahbi (G-7) (On top of NW bridge). She will tell you to wait for her at the Teahouse.
  • Return to Fari-Wari on the next game day (from the last time you spoke with him) for a cutscene and your reward.
