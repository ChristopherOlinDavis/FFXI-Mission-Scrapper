Walkthrough[]

  • At Beaucedine Glacier (S), visit the 5 northernmost Pso'Xja towers listed below, and examine the Regal Pawprints found there. Order does not matter, but in this walkthrough they are listed bottom-to-top. If you use the Survival Guide, reverse the order.
    • You will play a minigame at each tower. If you fail a particular minigame, you can just re-attempt it.
    • Monsters will aggro if not sneaked/invised at lvl 99 on the way to the Regal Pawprints. They will de-aggro if you get to a Regal Pawprint.
    • Note: The Regal Pawprints on Wiki map for Beaucedine Galcier [S] coincide with the next mission. Only two of the marks are at the towers. Make sure you examine the Pso'Xja tower pawprints.
  • Cait Sith Coig: Level 1 (J-8). (the level you zoned in from Beaucedine Glacier (S).)
    • She will walk or run between the two turrets randomly. Occasionally, she will pause in the middle.
    • Try to time the shot and push the button when she is on the way out, and you think she will pause
  • Cait Sith Seachd: Level 2 (I-7).
    • You must tell Lilisette when Cait Sith Seachd turns her back so she can move undetected.
    • The Cait Sith will turn at entirely random intervals. When she turns at a 45-degree angle however, the next turn will always be facing away. This is the very best time to move. You can move 2-4 (even 5 if you're risky) times when she finally does a 45-degree turn.
      • Extreme patience is recommended for this minigame.
    • When Lilisette puts her hands on her hips, she will descend two steps the next time you tell her to move.
  • Cait Sith Ceithir: Level 3 (G-9).
    • Like the previous game, however it is Portia's turn to sneak up to the Cait Sith Ceithir.
    • When Portia starts looking around anxiously, there will be a longer delay between your instruction and her moving. So wait untill she turns at a 45-degree angle as the next turn will always be facing away. This is the very best time to move.
  • Cait Sith Aon: Level 4 (H-8). (the level where Campaign battles take place)
    • Like the previous game, but this time it is your turn to sneak to the Cait Sith Aon
    • Same as the others when she turns at a 45-degree angle the next turn will always be facing away.
  • Cait Sith Tri: Level 6 (F-7).
    • You will transform into a chocobo chick, and need to lure in Cait Sith Tri within 60 seconds. To catch her attention you can either chirp spiritedly, or expose your back.
    • She seems to respond best to chirping right up until the last few paces, at which point you will need to turn your back.
    • You must choose the stun option as soon as it becomes available.
  • After successfully completing all five games, go to the tower on level 1 (H-10) and examine the Regal Pawprints by the tree for a cutscene.

Game Description

Mission Orders
Cait Sith Naoi is convinced a traitor walks among her kin. Unmasking the turncoat requires that you attach a "shadow bug" to each of the Cait Siths scattered throughout the Beaucedine Glacier.
