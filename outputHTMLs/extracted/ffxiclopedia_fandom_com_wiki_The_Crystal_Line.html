The Crystal Line Taulluque Metalworks (I-8)


This mission requires a certain level of Rank Bar. Complete some repeatable missions or trade 1 crystal to a Conquest Overseer. Talk to a Bastok Gate Guard to receive the mission.

  • Crystals can most easily be acquired by activating the related Records of Eminence quests for Bastok Missions upon their completion.

Summary: Guard -> Cid -> Faded Crystal -> Cid -> Ayame

Walkthrough[]

  1. (Metalworks)Talk to Cid (H-8).
  2. Trade a crystal to the Telepoint at the Crag of Dem (or Crag of Holla or Crag of Mea). It will become a Faded Crystal.
    • (Konschtat Highlands) The easiest Crag to walk to from Bastok is the Crag of Dem (I-7) in Konschtat Highlands, which can be reached via western North Gustaberg at (E-6). The three Crags can be warped to via the Teleport-Dem, Teleport-Holla, and Teleport-Mea spells, but only if you possess the Dem gate crystal, Holla gate crystal, or Mea gate crystal respectively.
      • It is highly recommended to check the Telepoint to obtain a Dem gate crystal.
  3. (Metalworks) Trade to Cid the Faded Crystal to obtain the C. L. Report.
    • Optional: Talk to Naji (J-8)
  4. (Metalworks) Talk to Ayame (K-7) inside the Cannonry in Metalworks to complete the mission.
    • You get additional dialogue if you spoke to Naji before Ayame.

Game Description

Mission Orders
Find a section of the strange structure on the Konschtat Highlands that drains the energy from crystals. Bring a faded crystal back to Chief Engineer Cid for analysis.
