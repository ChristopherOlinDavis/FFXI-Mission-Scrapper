Walkthrough[]

  • To start this mission, enter Lower Delkfutt's Tower for a cutscene after installing the Chains of Promathia expansion pack.
  • After the cutscene has finished, enter Upper Jeuno for another cutscene.
  • After the second cutscene finishes, speak to Monberaux (G-10, inside the Infirmary) for final cutscene and a Mysterious Amulet.
  • *Important note* There are still two more people in Jeuno that you will need to speak to during the next mission.

If you did not start this mission prior to the July 2023 Version Update you are given a choice to skip the opening cutscene. If you do, you must interact with the Tales' Beginning at Lower Delkfutt's Tower, First Floor (H-10) to progress this mission line.
