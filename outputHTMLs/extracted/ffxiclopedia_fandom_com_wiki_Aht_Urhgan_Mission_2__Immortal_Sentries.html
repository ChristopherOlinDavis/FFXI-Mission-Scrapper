Walkthrough[]

  • Watch the cutscene, in which you will be given a Supplies Package.
    • To complete the mission, you need to take the Supplies Package to one of the Assault staging points; any one of the five staging points will do. Eventually you will want to go to all of them; much like supply quests, you can only use the Chamber of Passage to get to a staging point after you've used the Runic Portal at that staging point.
    • You only need to do one of the staging points to complete mission 2.

Halvung[]

  1. (Al Zahbi) Zone into Bhaflau Thickets via (Al Zahbi G-6)
  2. (Bhaflau Thickets) From here enter a tunnel to zone into Halvung via (2) @ (Bhaflau Thickets F-8)
  3. (Halvung) After zoning in, head SW to I-9. and then north to the zone at H-7 to enter Mount Zhayolm @ (2)
    • (Escape also takes you right out to Mount Zhayolm.)
  4. (Mount Zhayolm) From (2), head East to L-8, North to L-6, West to K-7, then finally North to K-6 to reach the Heavy Iron Gate.
    • Inside the gate, talk to Waudeen for a cutscene and 150 Imperial Standing.

Azouph Isle[]

  1. (Aht Urghan Whitegate I-5) Take the ferry to Nashmau.
    • Alternatively, Unity Warp to Caedarva Mire drops you near Nashmau's eastern exit.
  2. (Nashmau H-6) Take the North exit to Caedarva Mire
  3. (Caedarva Mire K-9) Head to the Heavy Iron Gate.
    • Inside the gate, talk to Nareema for a cutscene and 150 Imperial Standing.

Ilrusi Atoll[]

  1. (Aht Urghan Whitegate I-5) Take the ferry to Nashmau.
  2. (Nashmau H-6) Take the North exit to Caedarva Mire
  3. (Caedarva Mire I-6) Zone into Arrapago Reef
  4. (Arrapago Reef J-10) Map 1 - You will need a Lamian Fang Key to open the iron gate
    • a thief can pick the lock with tools.
  5. (Arrapago Reef E-7) Map 1 - Make your way to the boat, marked "A", on the first map - this should put you on Map 2.
  6. (Arrapago Reef G-5) Map 2 - zone for a CS.
    • Beware of true sight imps in rooms right before the staging point gate.
  7. Inside the gate, talk to Meyaada for a cutscene and 150 Imperial Standing.

Dvucca Isle[]

  1. Take the ferry from Aht Urhgan Whitegate (I-5) to Nashmau.
  2. Head to Arrapago Reef and make way to E-7 (labeled 'A' on Vana'diel Atlas) as in Ilrusi Atoll staging point.
  3. Head to C-10 on second map (labeled 'C' on Vana'diel Atlas).
  4. Head to I-11 on third map (labeled 'H' on Vana'diel Atlas).
  5. Go through iron gate at H-11, then hug left wall to zone out to Caedarva Mire ("Appapago Reef" (sic) '2' labeled on Vana'diel Atlas).
  6. Hug left wall in Caedarva Mire to small clearing with staging point gate.

ALTERNATE ROUTE (Read easiest route ever to a staging point)

  1. Take the west exit out of Nashmau.
  2. Head west to Caedarva Mire (E-11), where there is an Immortal, Trade him an Imperial Silver Piece to enter the Alzadaal Undersea Ruins (#3 exit on the map).
  3. Proceed to the transportation platform past the gilded door. Use the device.
  4. Exit out to Caedarva Mire, Map 2 (I-10) (#2 entrance).
    • There is an Immortal guard here. If you intend to use a mount or Invisible , wait until you are past him.
  5. Head north hugging the east wall. You'll end up at the Heavy Iron door that leads to the staging point in no time.
  6. Talk to Nahshib for a cutscene and 150 Imperial Standing.

Mamool Ja[]

  1. Take Aht Urhgan Whitegate exit to Wajaom Woodlands. If besieged is going on you can take a tunnel next to the bottom of the stairs at H-10. It is the little tunnel on the left side of the south square where the ship from Mhaura docks.
  2. Alternatively, you can use the Survival Book to teleport into Wajaom Woodlands.
  3. Travel west, then south until E-12 and enter Mamook.
  4. Wind your way roughly south and west until F-10 and enter Bhaflau Thickets. Poroggos are true hearing, take care when crossing their paths.
  5. You will be on the southern half of the second map. Head south to H-11.
  6. Enter Imperial Compound through the Heavy Iron Gate.
  7. Talk to Daswil for a cutscene.

After the Staging Point[]

  • Use the Runic Portal to return to Aht Urhgan Whitegate.
  • Talk to Naja Salaheem again to finish this mission and start the next mission.

Game Description[]

Mission Orders:Deliver a package to one of the Immortals found at the staging points and Naja will consider you for a mercenary contract.
