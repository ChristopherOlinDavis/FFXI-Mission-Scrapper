Were you looking for Quests?

Missions are tasks that a player performs to advance in the main story lines of Final Fantasy XI. Rewards for missions vary. Missions for the three starting cities can reward the player with higher ranks, Gil, national flags, and the ability to purchase new items with Conquest Points. Missions from the expansion areas allow players to unlock previously inaccessible areas, gain special equipment, and sometimes participate in follow-up quests that expand upon the story lines.
