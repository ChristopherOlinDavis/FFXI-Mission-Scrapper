Series Treasures of Aht Urhgan
Starting NPC Halver, Chateau d'Oraguille - (I-9)
Title None
Repeatable No
Description An international summit is to be held in Jeuno. You have been called upon to speak of what you have witnessed in the Empire of Aht Urhgan.
Previous Mission Next Mission
Confessions of Royalty Westerly Winds
Rewards
Imperial Bronze Piece x10

Walkthrough

Note: You must wait one Earth minute after completing the previous quest to begin this quest.

  • Approach the Palace in Ru'Lude Gardens for a cutscene.
  • Choose any of the answers to the questions to complete the quest.
    • Choosing "Yes", followed by "That's what I'm here for!" will reward you with 10 Imperial Bronze Pieces.
    • Choosing "What am I, a donkey?" will complete the quest but forfeit 10x Imperial Bronze Piece
